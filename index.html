About the stay

A home in the countryside, just a short drive from the city

Wake up to birdsong, spend the afternoon by the pool, and end the day around a bonfire under the stars. The farmhouse is booked as a whole, so the entire property is yours: no shared spaces, no strangers.

Our caretaker lives on site and can arrange meals, barbecue, and local sightseeing on request.

  • ✔ Check-in 12 PM / Check-out 11 AM
  • ✔ Pet friendly on request
  • ✔ Free parking for 8 cars
